# Owner checklist for tomorrow

All implementation phases are prepared. These are the remaining owner inputs, in order. Enter secrets in the chosen host's environment settings, never Git or chat. Use `.env.example` as the variable list and `docs/operations/ENVIRONMENT.md` as the reference.

1. **Business identity and contact:** supply `NEXT_PUBLIC_LEGAL_NAME`, `NEXT_PUBLIC_PRIVACY_EMAIL`, monitored `NEXT_PUBLIC_BUSINESS_EMAIL` and optional `NEXT_PUBLIC_MAILING_ADDRESS`. Confirm `(833) 696-9996` or replace the two phone variables. Set `NEXT_PUBLIC_PHONE_VERIFIED=true` and `NEXT_PUBLIC_EMAIL_VERIFIED=true` only for verified contacts.
2. **Privacy, terms and retention:** review `docs/legal/PRIVACY_REVIEW_DRAFT.md` and `TERMS_REVIEW_DRAFT.md`; approve the public EN/FR text. Set the same approved day count in `LEAD_RETENTION_DAYS` and `NEXT_PUBLIC_RETENTION_DAYS`, plus the actual `NEXT_PUBLIC_DELIVERY_PROVIDER_LABEL`. Set `PRIVACY_POLICY_APPROVED=true` only after approval. Arrange actual inbox/CRM retention; the website cannot delete those external records.
3. **Inquiry delivery:** choose Resend or the documented authenticated webhook; enter `LEAD_PROVIDER`, `LEAD_FROM_EMAIL`, `LEAD_TO_EMAIL` and its secret credentials. Generate a random secret of at least 32 characters for `LEAD_SIGNING_SECRET`; set a persistent absolute `LEAD_RATE_LIMIT_DIR`. Follow `docs/operations/INQUIRY_DELIVERY.md`. Authorize one internal delivery test before setting `OUTBOUND_DELIVERY_AUTHORIZED=true` and `INQUIRIES_ENABLED=true`. CRM is optional and remains off unless separately configured/authorized.
4. **Business and supplier accuracy:** confirm delivery/installation areas, rental/service availability and the EN/FR planning copy. Supply evidence for any desired product specifications, accessibility/certification claims, supplier relationships and licensed photos using `docs/suppliers/WORKFLOW.md`. Current pages avoid asserting unverified models, inventory or completed installations. Every new image placement needs a unique asset and register entry.
5. **Hosting configuration:** WHC hosting, domain DNS, HTTPS and canonical redirects are connected. Supply the approved public settings and private inquiry credentials above in the existing WHC Node application; rebuild for public setting changes. See `docs/operations/HOSTING_WHC.md` for the deployed configuration.
6. **Final approval on your devices:** review French wording, quote/download flows and the manual screen-reader/keyboard checklist in `docs/qa/ACCESSIBILITY.md`. Follow `docs/operations/LAUNCH_VERIFICATION.md` for staging, the authorized internal inbox test and final domain checks. Search Console and analytics are optional owner account decisions in that guide.
