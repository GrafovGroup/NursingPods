## Usage and verification

Each image appears once: English hero and French hero respectively. No category imagery, fallback imagery, duplicate social-card imagery or image backgrounds are introduced. Responsive sizes generated by Next.js serve the same single placement. Both outputs were visually inspected. Per owner instruction, no customer-facing provenance caption is added; no claims identify these as supplier products or past projects.

The automated check compares SHA-256 and 256-bit difference hashes against accessible local sibling-project images. This is a candidate-screening check, not proof that every image on all remote sites is unique. Remote inventories and unmounted assets remain unverified. This limit is internal and must not appear on the business website.
