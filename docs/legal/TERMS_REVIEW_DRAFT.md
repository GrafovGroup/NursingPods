# Internal commercial terms draft — owner and legal review required

The public website describes planning information and the inquiry process. It does not publish a sales contract, payment flow or invented warranty. Before issuing binding proposals, obtain an approved EN/FR agreement covering the actual business model.

Owner/reviewer decisions:

- Legal contracting entity and contact address.
- Who sells, supplies, installs and services each item; do not assume supplier or contractor relationships.
- Proposal validity, acceptance method, payment/tax terms, cancellation and changes.
- Delivery scope, unloading, site readiness, building approvals and responsibilities for regulated work.
- Product-specific warranties, exclusions and support confirmed by the actual supplier.
- Rental dates, return condition, cleaning, damage and collection responsibilities if rentals are actually offered.
- Applicable dispute/contract provisions and any required French documentation, assessed by the reviewer for the actual transaction.
- Image/content usage rights, privacy references and accessibility representations supported by evidence.

## Points à faire approuver en français

Confirmer l’entité contractante, les rôles de fourniture et d’installation, la validité et l’acceptation des propositions, les paiements et taxes, les modifications et annulations. Définir la livraison, la manutention, la préparation des lieux et les travaux réglementés. Vérifier les garanties réelles et, pour une location, les dates, le nettoyage, les dommages et la reprise. Faire examiner les dispositions contractuelles et la documentation française selon l’activité réelle. Aucune clause de ce document n’est présentée comme approuvée ou suffisante juridiquement.
