# National location coverage

Coverage baseline: all 41 census metropolitan areas in Statistics Canada's 2021 geographic classification, plus Charlottetown, Whitehorse, Yellowknife and Iqaluit. Existing Laval and Longueuil editorial pages remain separate, for 47 metro/capital entries across all 13 provinces and territories. This is an explicit editorial baseline, not a claim that every Canadian municipality has a standalone page.

Source: https://www23.statcan.gc.ca/imdb/p3VD.pl?CLV=1&CPV=A&CST=01012021&CVD=1348194&Function=getVD&MLV=3&TVD=1348193 (accessed 2026-10-18).

Nearby-community lists describe project inquiry areas, not exact census boundaries. Boroughs and neighbourhoods can appear alongside municipalities to help visitors identify the actual project destination. They do not imply local stock, offices, installations, delivery guarantees or completed projects.

English search intent: nursing pods + city; lactation rooms + city; workplace lactation rooms; private breastfeeding spaces; nursing-room design; installation coordination. French intent: cabines d’allaitement + ville; salles d’allaitement au travail; espaces privés d’allaitement; aménagement de salles d’allaitement. Use these in meaningful titles, summaries and project-planning sections, without keyword stuffing or fabricated local facts.

Each metro brief has a distinct planning scenario and decision guidance in English and French. Province/territory directories link to their regional pages. Surrounding communities prefill the quote form; they are not duplicate doorway pages. Canonicals, equivalent-language links and sitemap entries are generated from the same records. Search ranking and indexing are not guaranteed; Search Console ownership remains an owner account decision.
